A little note for your birthday

My dearest Fahmeetah,

Happiest birthday to my favourite built-in best friend, my partner in memories, and the person who can annoy me in two seconds and make me smile the next! ❤️

Looking at our pictures makes me realise how quickly time has flown. There we were, two little girls making memories without even knowing how precious those moments would become. And now look at us — growing up, changing, collecting new stories, but still carrying that same sisterly bond with us.

We've had our share of silly fights, teasing, laughing for no reason, disagreeing over the smallest things, and probably annoying each other more than anyone else could. 😂 Sometimes I may act like you're the most irritating person ever, but let's be honest — life would be so boring without you. Who else would I laugh with, argue with, share random things with, and make all these unforgettable memories?

Jokes apart, I want you to know how much you mean to me. You are a very special part of my life, and I'm genuinely grateful that I get to call you my sister. No matter where life takes us, how busy we become, or how much we grow, I hope we never lose this bond. You will always have a place in my heart that nobody else can ever take.

On your birthday, I wish you endless happiness, good health, peace, confidence, and success in everything you dream of doing. I hope you get all the beautiful things you quietly wish for. May you always find reasons to smile, strength when things get difficult, and people who love and value you just as you are.

Keep being your lovely, crazy, wonderful self. Keep smiling, keep shining, and keep making memories with me. We still have so many pictures to take, places to go, jokes to share, and little arguments to have — so don't think I'm done annoying you anytime soon! 😂

Happy birthday once again, Fahmeetah.
I may not say it every day, but I love you more than all our silly fights can ever show. Always your sister, always on your side. ♡

With lots of love (and a little bit of teasing),
Your forever person ♡
